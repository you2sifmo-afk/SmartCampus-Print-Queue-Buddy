## 1. Executive Summary
University print shops face severe peak-hour bottlenecks between classes, resulting in student tardiness, wasted academic time, and operational chaos for shop operators. **SmartCampus Print & Queue Buddy** is a cloud-assisted micro-queuing solution designed using Design Thinking and AI acceleration to digitize order placement, automate queue tracking, and remove daily physical friction.

## 2. Empathy Portfolio (Observation & Field Research)

### 2.1 User Interview Transcripts
* **Student Tester 1 (Sophomore - Engineering):**
  > *"Every Monday morning I wait 20 to 25 minutes just to print a 3-page lab report. Half the time, the printer runs out of paper right before my turn, making me late to class and losing attendance points."*
* **Student Tester 2 (Freshman - Computer Science):**
  > *"Sharing files via personal WhatsApp or flash drives at the counter feels chaotic and insecure. The operator gets mixed up with copies, and we waste time re-verifying payments."*
* **Print Shop Operator:**
  > *"Students swarm the counter all at once during the 15-minute breaks. Everyone is searching their phones for documents, internet signals drop, and lines extend into the hallway."*

### 2.2 Current User Journey Map
1. **Arrival:** High friction, stress due to approaching class deadlines and unpredictable line length.
2. **Waiting:** Average unproductive wait time of 15–25 minutes without status visibility.
3. **File Transfer:** Manual transfers prone to transmission errors and privacy concerns.
4. **Fulfillment:** Confusion over pickup order and manual cash/UPI confirmation delays.
